All four hands face up, not rated. You call and play for every chair the nets do not (all four by default); the hints follow the chair on turn and see only what it could see. The address bar is the position; edit it to set one up: /debug#n=AQJ32.Q8.T32.K83&e=…&s=…&w=…&dealer=E&vul=ns&auction=1S-P-2C-P&play=HK-HA&hints=1. Add seat=S to hold South only, or bots=EW to give the nets some chairs. A hand left out is dealt at random. A new dealer or vulnerability starts the auction again.
