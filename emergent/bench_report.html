Where the IMPs go

Contested = both sides made a call other than Pass at that table. "Of match" = IMPs per board of the whole match.

Doubles

A double is a double here: takeout and penalty look the same. Level 1–2 doubles are mostly takeout.

Competing on: value of the last outbid

When a side made the last outbid: IMPs against letting the other side play its last bid undoubled, by the outbidding pair's combined HCP. Negative with few HCP = bids on too light.

Bidding style

Artificial = a call partner cannot read at face value: a suit bid without 4 cards (or 3 in partner's suit), a double at level 1–3, a redouble, a 2♣ opening that is not clubs.

Openings

Contracts are scored double-dummy: every declarer and defender plays perfectly, which flatters the defence against doubled partscores most. Fewer than about 2,000 boards leave wide ranges.
