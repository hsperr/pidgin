# Server backlog

Ideas for future work on the playable table. These are proposals, not scheduled features.

## Playing and scoring

- Allow a player to claim some remaining tricks, such as three of the last five. Claims currently cover all remaining tricks.
- Allow claims during challenges. Claims are currently disabled there because a rejected claim could reveal what the defending bot can win.

## Accounts and competition

- Add accounts so a player can keep their rating across browsers and devices. The current rating is tied to a browser cookie.
- Award the Champion theme for a defined season. The current leaderboard awards it to the current leader after at least eight boards.
- Check leaderboard names before displaying them publicly.

## Table appearance

The current theme picker applies one card back and table mat to the whole table. Preview the existing themes in [`design/themes.html`](design/themes.html).

- Let each player choose the card back shown at their seat.
- Explore new artwork for face cards at larger card sizes.
- Let players design and share card backs and table mats, with a review step before others can use them.
